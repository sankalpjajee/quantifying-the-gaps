\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \method, a plug-and-play system for persona-based user simulation for evaluating interactive applications. Across survey, chatbot, and web settings, \method shows application-level differences, persona-group variation, and persona alignment patterns. These findings position \method as a useful tool for simulating real-user studies of interactive systems. Future work should calibrate simulations against human data and expand to broader domain of applications.
