\section{Related Work}
 \label{sec:related-personas} 
 
%  \paragraph{Persona datasets and conditioned generation.} Persona-grounded modeling began with small, hand-written profile sets such as PersonaChat~\cite{zhang2018personachat} and its realistic-personality variant RealPersonaChat~\cite{realpersonachat2023}, and has since scaled by orders of magnitude through synthetic generation. Persona-Hub mines roughly one billion personas from web data as ``distributed carriers of world knowledge''~\cite{ge2024personahub}; NVIDIA Nemotron-Personas anchors one million profiles to U.S. census distributions via a probabilistic graphical model followed by LLM narrative expansion~\cite{nvidia2025nemotron}; DeepPersona pushes per-persona depth to 200+ attributes through a taxonomy mined from real user--ChatGPT conversations~\cite{deeppersona2025}; and SynthLabs PERSONA procedurally combines census demographics with idiosyncratic attributes for pluralistic-alignment testing~\cite{castricato2024persona}. A parallel line synthesizes persona-grounded \emph{dialogue} and \emph{behavior} data: Synthetic-Persona-Chat uses a generator--critic loop~\cite{jandaghi2023syntheticpersonachat}, OpenCharacter converts personas into role-play training data~\cite{wang2025opencharacter}, and self-supervised recipes such as Self-Instruct~\cite{wang2023selfinstruct}, Evol-Instruct/WizardLM~\cite{xu2023wizardlm}, Character-LLM~\cite{shao2023characterllm} and Ditto~\cite{lu2024ditto} bake persona-conditioned supervision into model weights. Conditioning can also be elicited rather than written---through first-person backstory anthologies~\cite{moon2024anthology}, character training on self-generated preference data~\cite{anthropic2024character}, or activation-space steering with persona vectors~\cite{chen2025personavectors}. Crucially, recent work argues that demographic descriptors alone are weak behavioral predictors: SCOPE finds that demographics explain only $\sim$1.5\% of human-response variance and that sociopsychological factors (values, beliefs, identity) substantially improve behavioral fidelity~\cite{wang2025scope}, echoing the interview-grounded agents of Park et al.~\cite{park2024thousand} and motivating coverage-oriented generators that span rare, long-tail profiles~\cite{deepmind2026personagenerators,tan2025populationaligned}.

\paragraph{Persona datasets.} Persona-grounded modeling has evolved from small manually written profiles such as PersonaChat~\citep{zhang2018personachat} to large-scale synthetic persona datasets. Recent efforts have generated millions to billions of personas from web data, demographic priors, and LLM synthesis, including Persona-Hub~\citep{ge2024personahub}, NVIDIA Nemotron-Personas~\citep{nvidia2025nemotron}, and DeepPersona~\citep{deeppersona2025}. Parallel work has developed methods for persona-conditioned dialogue and behavior generation through synthetic data and instruction tuning~\citep{jandaghi2023syntheticpersonachat,wang2025opencharacter,wang2023selfinstruct}. 
% More recent studies further suggest that behaviorally relevant factors, such as values, beliefs, and identity, are more predictive than demographics alone~\citep{wang2025scope,park2024thousand}. Together, these advances provide increasingly rich persona representations that can serve as inputs to downstream simulation and evaluation systems.


% \paragraph{Persona simulation, evaluation, and its failure modes.} Personas become useful when instantiated as agents that behave faithfully over time. The generative-agent architecture of memory, reflection, and planning~\cite{park2023generative} underpins simulation substrates such as Concordia~\cite{vezhnevets2023concordia}, OASIS~\cite{yang2024oasis}, TinyTroupe~\cite{microsoft2024tinytroupe}, AI Town~\cite{a16z2023aitown}, and economic large-population models~\cite{zhou2025llmeconomist}. A complementary strand measures \emph{how faithfully} agents follow a persona: PersonaGym~\cite{samuel2024personagym}, RoleLLM~\cite{wang2023rolellm}, CharacterEval~\cite{tu2024charactereval}, RoleEval~\cite{shen2023roleeval}, PersonaLLM~\cite{personallm2024} and SimBench~\cite{simbench2025} score adherence, knowledge, and group-level behavioral realism, while $\tau$-bench~\cite{yao2024taubench} and ``beyond-cooperative'' user simulators~\cite{joshi2026beyondcooperative} stress-test agents against realistic, non-cooperative users. The field is bounded by well-documented failure modes that directly shape our data-collection priorities: the ``silicon-sampling'' fidelity criterion~\cite{argyle2023oneofmany} is undercut by demographic misalignment~\cite{santurkar2023whoseopinions} and by the flattening of within-group diversity into stereotypes~\cite{wang2024flatten}; structured chat formatting induces \emph{diversity collapse}~\cite{west2025priceofformat}; and assigned personas \emph{drift} over multi-turn dialogue~\cite{li2024instability}. The survey of role-playing language agents by Chen et al.~\cite{chen2024personasurvey} organizes this space into demographic, character, and individualized personas and frames the construction-and-evaluation pipeline that MatrAIx targets at population scale.

\paragraph{Persona simulation and evaluation.} Persona-grounded agents have enabled increasingly realistic simulations through frameworks such as Concordia~\citep{vezhnevets2023concordia}, OASIS~\citep{yang2024oasis}, TinyTroupe~\citep{microsoft2024tinytroupe}, and AI Town~\citep{a16z2023aitown}. Parallel work evaluates whether agents faithfully follow assigned personas using benchmarks such as PersonaGym, CharacterEval, SimBench, and $\tau$-bench~\citep{samuel2024personagym,tu2024charactereval,simbench2025,yao2024taubench}. Our work is complementary to these efforts by providing a modular simulation platform that conditions recommendation agents on arbitrary persona profiles, enabling systematic evaluation across diverse personas and applications.



% \paragraph{Applications: survey, chatbot, and web.} Persona agents are increasingly deployed as proxies for human respondents. In the \emph{survey} setting, an agent reads a stimulus and returns structured feedback: large-scale replications find that LLMs recover 73--81\% of main effects from human experiments, albeit with inflated effect sizes and weaker fidelity on sensitive topics~\citep{Cui+2024}. In the \emph{chatbot} setting, an agent converses with a target system and evaluates it, most developed for conversational recommendation: off-the-shelf LLMs are strong zero-shot recommenders~\citep{He+2023}, and the central shift is toward interactive, simulator-driven evaluation, since static single-turn protocols substantially underestimate quality~\citep{Wang+2023} and simulators exhibit failure modes such as popularity bias~\citep{Yoon+2024}. In the \emph{web} setting, agents probe products and interfaces before any human study, generating thousands of testers for live websites~\citep{Lu+2025a}, populating prototype social spaces~\citep{Park+2022}, and running A/B tests on real shopping interfaces~\citep{Lu+2025b}.
